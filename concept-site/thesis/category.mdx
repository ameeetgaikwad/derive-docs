# Outcome-driven markets for held assets

Hedge begins with a simple observation: ownership creates intentions that spot markets do not capture. A holder may already know the future price at which they would trim, the lower price at which they would add, or the period during which they want a different risk profile.

Those intentions can become markets.

## The category definition

An **outcome-driven market** starts with a result the holder can describe, translates it into a bounded financial request, invites specialist counterparties to price it, and enforces the accepted outcome under shared rules.

That sequence is different from placing a professional trading interface in front of a retail holder. The product owns the translation layer:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="State the goal" icon="message-square-text">
    Begin with an amount, target, and time horizon that make sense in the holder's own portfolio.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Price the outcome" icon="badge-dollar-sign">
    Let market makers compete on the same clearly defined request.

  </Card>

## Why the pieces can be composed

The model needs held inventory that can back an obligation, external prices that can anchor risk and expiry, specialist liquidity that can quote a bespoke request, and programmable settlement that cannot change the terms afterward.

Hedge's strategic opportunity is to compose those pieces behind one understandable decision, prove the loop with BTC covered calls, and reuse the market engine only where the next outcome meets the same evidence standard.
