# Milestones, not calendar promises

The roadmap is organized around evidence. Each stage should end with a stronger reason to trust the next one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="1. Close the production gates" icon="shield-check">
    Finish the pricing and collateral controls, external review, ownership transition, durable service deployment, settlement rehearsals, maker onboarding, frontend verification, and operating playbooks required by the production checklist.
  </Step>
  <Step title="2. Launch under controlled limits" icon="gauge">
    Begin with conservative exposure caps, a narrow market set, monitored quote and fill quality, explicit pause conditions, and a rehearsed recovery path. Replace assumptions with observed operating data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="3. Prove repeat use" icon="repeat-2">
    Measure whether holders understand the trade-off, makers compete reliably, accepted quotes settle cleanly, and customers return for another outcome.
  </Step>
  <Step title="4. Expand the engine" icon="blocks">
    Add a market or outcome only when its price source, collateral model, maker coverage, settlement behavior, and distribution story meet the same evidence standard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The milestone that matters next

The next company-level milestone is not a broad launch announcement. It is a controlled, production-approved loop in which a real holder request receives competitive quotes, executes under final controls, settles correctly, and produces observable operating evidence.

That milestone would validate three things at once:

- The holder framing is understandable enough to create qualified intent.
- The market is attractive enough for makers to price consistently.
- The system is reliable enough to turn that intent into a completed outcome.

<Warning>
This roadmap is directional. It contains no promised dates, market counts, revenue targets, or production availability claims.
</Warning>
