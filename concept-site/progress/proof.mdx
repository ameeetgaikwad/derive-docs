# Evidence with the boundary left intact

Hedge has moved beyond a paper design: the repository contains the product flow, market-maker coordination, on-chain risk and settlement rails, oracle services, and isolated chain-56 staging configuration. A minimal-value BTCB staging trade has completed.

That evidence matters because it connects the narrative to a working system. It does not erase the remaining launch gates.

<Warning>
**Hedge is PRE-LAUNCH.** Chain 56 is an isolated, capped staging environment for explicitly labelled public testing. The tracked staging manifest spans **BTC, Gold, the S&P 500, and NVIDIA** as staging-manifest markets; this is not a claim that every market is continuously public or production-live. **Production deposits and unrestricted production trading remain gated.** Stale RWA sources fail closed, and the equity-market calendar is not a promise of continuous pricing.
</Warning>

## What has been demonstrated

- A holder flow can express a covered-call outcome and receive competing maker quotes.
- Signed terms can be resolved through on-chain execution and risk checks.
- The tracked staging configuration spans crypto, metal, and equity-referenced markets.
- A minimal-value BTCB staging trade has completed on real chain-56 rails.

## What is still a launch gate

- Production-grade collateral and stable-asset pricing.
- Focused external review of the customized risk, oracle, settlement, and RFQ paths.
- Production ownership, signing, monitoring, durable services, and recovery controls.
- Complete in-the-money and out-of-the-money settlement rehearsals.
- Production market-maker onboarding and an observable soak period.
- Final frontend, cap, pause, and rollback review.

<Info>
The honest proof point is not “live.” It is that the wedge and platform rails can now be tested as a connected system under deliberately constrained conditions.
</Info>
