# Turn a price target into a market

Hedge is building an outcome-driven market for people who already own an asset. Instead of asking them to become options traders, Hedge starts with a decision they already understand: **At what price would you be willing to sell?**

The first expression is a BTC covered call. A holder chooses an amount, a target price, and a date. Market makers compete to pay for the upside above that target. The holder sees the trade-off before accepting: premium now in exchange for giving up gains above the chosen level through expiry.

## The company-level thesis

Long-term holders are not one homogeneous audience. Some want maximum upside. Others already have a price at which they would trim, diversify, or rebalance. Hedge turns that latent intention into a competitive market without forcing the holder to navigate an exchange built for professional derivatives desks.
