# The wedge proves a reusable market engine

BTC covered calls are the first packaged outcome. Underneath them sits a broader set of rails that can originate a request, invite competing prices, enforce risk, move positions and cash together, and resolve the result against an external reference.

Those capabilities can be reused without forcing every future product into the same customer story.

## The five reusable capabilities

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Intent" icon="message-square-text">
    Turn a human goal into a bounded request with an amount, target, and time window.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Price discovery" icon="gavel">
    Let specialist counterparties compete for the same request rather than quote against an opaque house price.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Risk" icon="shield-check">
    Check collateral and obligations under consistent rules before a trade can alter balances.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Execution" icon="waypoints">
    Bind both sides to the accepted terms and apply the position and cash legs together.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Settlement" icon="calendar-check">
    Resolve time-bound outcomes from defined price references, with stale inputs treated as a stop condition.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Expansion without losing focus

The platform can expand along three independent axes:

- **More held assets:** reuse the same core rails with asset-specific collateral and price controls.
- **More outcomes:** package future sell targets, future buy targets, and other bounded risk preferences.
- **More distribution:** present the same market engine through experiences designed for different holders or partners.

These are directions, not launch promises. Each new market still needs trustworthy pricing, settlement rules, maker participation, operating controls, and a customer story that is simpler than the mechanism behind it.

<Note>
The defensibility thesis is operational: repeatably connecting understandable holder intent to competitive specialist liquidity and reliable settlement across markets.
</Note>
