# Monetization follows completed market activity

Hedge includes a transaction-linked protocol fee. When a fill creates new open interest, each side pays a configurable cash fee to the configured fee account.

This is a mechanism already represented in the system—not a claim of realized revenue. The production rate and minimum are not final, so this narrative does not present a fixed take rate or forecast.

## The alignment

The fee is tied to a matched position being created. That makes the commercial question concrete: can Hedge originate outcomes that holders value and that market makers are willing to price?

## The potential compounding loop

1. A simpler outcome attracts more qualified requests.
2. Better request quality gives makers clearer flow to price.
3. Stronger competition can improve the offers holders see.
4. Reliable execution and settlement make the loop repeatable.
5. A proven loop can be adapted to the next suitable market.

This is the operating hypothesis. It should be measured with verified funnel, quote, fill, retention, and settlement data after controlled launch—not asserted in advance.

## What the model claims

The current case does not promise a fixed yield or a fixed take rate. It describes one reviewed commercial mechanism: create and settle a market around a holder's chosen outcome, then charge according to configured transaction economics.
