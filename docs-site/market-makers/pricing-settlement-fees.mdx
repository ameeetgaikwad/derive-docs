## On-chain feeds (the protocol's marks)

**BTC spot now comes from a Pyth adapter cross-checked against Chainlink**; the remaining feeds (forward, vol, rate, USDT stable) are on-chain signed feeds posted by the protocol's oracle-feeds service (testnet: 1-of-1 signer `0xdE50B8E965aD2B8E25f45a19FD87A2d2c737782F` — **testnet only**). You can read everything directly; all return 18dp values plus a confidence:

| Feed | Address (97) | Read |
|---|---|---|
| BTC spot (**PythSpotFeed**, live SRM spot feed) | `0xaAc2C29105928A6fe7788956058bcF3B9A3D5E51` | `getSpot() -> (uint price, uint conf)` |
| BTC spot (signed — kept as fallback) | `0x69662A47C3C2626EB75a8c861C48a0a87Cb01b2C` | `getSpot() -> (uint, uint)` |
| BTC forward | `0x86b7148B69F3eFad27Af6d0d892d063D6a6C9e05` | `getForwardPrice(uint64 expiry) -> (uint, uint)` |
| BTC vol (SVI surface) | `0x5a892364cFBd8e725eC1e7af25855005C0E0f0aE` | `getVol(uint128 strike, uint64 expiry) -> (uint, uint)` |
| BTC rate | `0x11C394e92592d08B43fE957c1A80f4c1F012d15D` | `getInterestRate(uint64 expiry) -> (int, uint)` |
| USDT/USD stable | `0x2Eeb9512F40c1964eCA5dc156D8e2479dA632116` | `getSpot() -> (uint, uint)` |

The spot adapter reads Pyth's `Crypto.BTC/USD` from the official Pyth contract on BSC testnet, **reverts if the Pyth price is older than 60 s**, and zeroes the returned confidence if Chainlink BTC/USD deviates by more than 1% (tripping the SRM's oracle-contingency margin penalty). Full mechanics in [Oracles & feeds](/reference/oracles).

<Warning>
**Pyth is a pull oracle.** Quoting/trading requires a recent on-chain push — a stale Pyth price (>60 s) makes the spot feed revert, which fails margin checks and therefore fills. Run `pnpm --filter @hedge/oracle-feeds pyth-push` before triggering test trades (any funded key; it only pays gas). See the [runbook](/market-makers/runbook).
</Warning>

The reference bot prices Black-76 on `forward`/`vol`/`rate` exactly from these reads, falling back to `getSpot()` when an expiry has no forward data yet. Price with your own marks by all means — but note that **margin and the OI fee are computed from *these* feeds**, and the engine's cash pre-check uses your quoted premium.

<Note>
On testnet the surface is currently a flat 60% IV SVI fit and the signed feeds update only when posted — don't expect live-market dynamics. ABIs for all feeds ship in the TypeScript SDK (`@hedge/shared`).
</Note>

## Settlement at expiry

- Options are **European, cash-settled in USDT** against the on-chain settlement price; the taker's BTCB stays in their subaccount as collateral (it is not delivered to you).
- Sequence: at/after expiry the forward feed receives settlement data fixing the expiry price; then **anyone** may call `StandardManager.settleOptions(optionAsset, subaccountId)` per account. The protocol's ops run this routinely, but you can settle your own subaccount permissionlessly once the fix is posted.
- Your long call settles to `max(F_settle − K, 0) × size` credited as cash; the short side is debited the same. Until settlement is triggered, the expired position just sits in the subaccount.
- The fix on testnet comes from the forward feed's settlement-window TWAP aggregates posted by the feed signer; there is no external settlement auction yet.

See the [settlement reference](/reference/settlement) for the taker-side view.

## Fees you pay

Two layers exist; only one is non-zero today.

### 1. Matching/module fee — currently always 0

`FillData.makerFee` is capped by the `maxFee` you sign. **The engine currently always submits 0.** Sign `maxFee: 0` and you are guaranteed zero module fee (`RFQM_FeeTooHigh` protects you on-chain).

### 2. SRM open-interest (OI) fee

Charged by the StandardManager inside the trade transaction whenever a trade **increases open interest** in a subId (every new covered call does), per side, in cash, to the fee-recipient subaccount (id **3** on testnet):

```text
fee = |amount| × forwardPrice(expiry) × OIFeeRateBPS     (all 18dp decimal math)
charged = max(fee, minOIFee)        per participant, only if subId OI increased
```

As currently deployed on chain 97: `OIFeeRateBPS = 0.001e18` and `minOIFee = 10e18`, i.e. **`max(0.1% of forward notional, 10 USDT)` per side**. Despite the parameter's "BPS" name it is a plain 18dp decimal multiplier.

<Note>
The original deploy shipped a `0.1e18` (10%) placeholder — that is the 6,279 USDT fee visible in the historical [worked example](/market-makers/worked-example); it was fixed on-chain on 2026-06-12. The rate remains **governance-settable** — **do not hard-code it.** Read the live values on-chain:

```text
SRMPortfolioViewer.OIFeeRateBPS(btcOptionAsset)   // viewer: 0xbC9ae813B45Fa950AabE2E82eAB023Dd45a36486
StandardManager.minOIFee()
```
</Note>

The OI fee **is included** in the engine's off-chain cash pre-check (estimated live from the same on-chain reads on every quote — see [Onboarding](/market-makers/onboarding#margin-what-buying-a-call-requires)), and it is charged in the on-chain margin transaction — your subaccount must absorb `premium + OI fee` and still pass initial margin.

### Gas

Zero for quoting and fills (the executor pays); you pay gas only for your own deposits/withdrawals.
