## What you need

<Steps>
  <Step title="One EOA private key">
    In v1 this single key does everything: authenticates your WS session, owns your subaccount, and signs quotes. The protocol contracts support session keys (`ActionVerifier.registerSessionKey`), but the RFQ engine **rejects** quotes with `signer != owner` (`v1 requires signer == owner (no session keys)`) — plan for a single hot key for now.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allowlist registration">
    The engine enforces a **maker allowlist** (`MAKER_ALLOWLIST`): register your maker EOA address with the team before connecting. An address that isn't allowlisted fails the WS auth handshake with `auth failed: address not allowlisted` and close code **4003** — see the [wire protocol](/market-makers/wire-protocol#auth-handshake-maker-ws). (Engines run with an empty allowlist — dev/local only — accept any address.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="tBNB for gas (setup only)">
    Gas is only needed for setup transactions (subaccount creation, approvals, deposits) and any direct withdrawals. **Quoting itself is gasless for you** — trades are submitted on-chain by the engine's executor key. Use any BSC testnet faucet; the full smoke-test setup cost ~0.02 tBNB per participant.
  </Step>
  <Step title="USDT cash collateral">
    Deposited into a Matching subaccount — see below.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Creating a subaccount under Matching

The account model: `SubAccounts` is an ERC-721 ledger; each subaccount NFT holds asset balances and is risk-checked by its manager (the **StandardManager / SRM**). To trade through the RFQ system your subaccount NFT must be **deposited into the Matching contract**, which records you as the off-chain owner (`Matching.subAccountToOwner[id] == your EOA`). The engine verifies this on-chain for every quote.

The easy path is the reference bot's `--setup` command, which performs exactly:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the subaccount">
    `Matching.createSubAccount(standardManager)` — creates the subaccount with the NFT held by Matching and you recorded as owner. Returns your `subaccountId`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    `USDT.approve(cashAsset, amount)`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit cash">
    `CashAsset.deposit(subaccountId, amount)` — pulls USDT from your EOA and credits 18dp "cash" to the subaccount. Deposit is permissionless for any recipient account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The bot persists the subaccount ID to `maker-state.<chainId>.json` (overridable with `MAKER_SUBACCOUNT_ID` / `MAKER_STATE_FILE`). You can equally do these three calls yourself — **no permission from the protocol is required for any of them**.

## Margin: what buying a call requires

Manager: StandardManager (SRM) at `0x4d55A929e184fc366664C11526E3B54aB70340B5`.

- A **long call is fully paid-for**: it requires no maintenance margin of its own. Your constraint is that after paying `premium + fees`, your subaccount must still pass the SRM **initial margin** check (≥ 0) in the trade transaction.
- USDT cash is the natural collateral. A negative cash balance is a borrow against other collateral (the taker side does exactly this — see the [worked example](/market-makers/worked-example)), but as a USDT-only maker you simply need enough cash.
- The engine additionally pre-checks **off-chain at quote time** that your cash balance covers the full cost of the quote, **including fees and your other outstanding quotes**:

  ```text
  price * amount / 1e18  +  maxFee  +  estimated SRM OI fee
    +  cash already reserved by your other live quotes
  ```

  rejecting otherwise (`insufficient maker cash: balance X < required Y (incl. OI fee Z) + reserved R across open quotes`).

<Note>
The OI fee estimate uses the same math as the chain (see [Pricing, settlement & fees](/market-makers/pricing-settlement-fees#fees-you-pay)), with `OIFeeRateBPS`, the forward price, and `minOIFee` read live on every quote — a failed feed read rejects the quote (`cannot estimate SRM OI fee ...`). Reserved cash is tracked per maker subaccount across **all your open auctions plus won-quotes awaiting taker accept**, and is released on cancel/replace/loss/execution/failure/deadline-expiry — concurrent auctions can no longer over-commit your balance at admission time. The pre-check is still a snapshot of your on-chain balance, so keep a buffer.
</Note>

### Useful reads for monitoring (all 18dp)

```text
SubAccounts.getBalance(subaccountId, cashAsset, 0)            // signed cash balance
SubAccounts.getBalance(subaccountId, btcOptionAsset, subId)   // option position
StandardManager.getMargin(subaccountId, true)                 // initial margin (SRM)
```
